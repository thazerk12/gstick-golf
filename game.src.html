How to play

  • SWINGPress anywhere in the lower half of the screen. Drag straight down to set power, then flick straight back up past where you started.
  • SHAPEYour flick is the club path of a right-handed swing. Drift right on the way up and the ball starts right and draws left. Drift left and it starts left and fades or slices right.
  • TEMPOA slow upswing loses distance. Pulling into the red zone past 100% adds yards but sprays the ball.
  • AIMDrag left or right in the upper half, or open Map and tap where you want to go. The ring shows full-power carry with no wind.
  • LIESRough costs distance and spin. Bunkers cost more unless you take a wedge. Water and out of bounds cost a stroke.
  • PUTTThe grid and drifting dots show the slope; dots roll downhill, so play above the hole. The putter's stroke length steps up with the putt and is shown under the club name. Dead-weight putts fall in off the lip; firm ones need to be centered.
  • FASTHold anywhere while the ball is moving to fast-forward.

Keyboard: ← → aim (hold Shift for fine), ↑ ↓ club, M map, Space fast-forward, Esc pause. Trackpad scroll also aims.
