# G$tick Golf: engineering handoff (after beta round 6)

A 3D physics golf game in the spirit of Golf Dreams, built for a phone browser (portrait first) and playable on desktop.

- **Live site:** https://thazerk12.github.io/gstick-golf/ (GitHub Pages, from the root of `main` in `thazerk12/gstick-golf`). It installs to the iPhone home screen as an app.
- **Outside loads:** only Google Fonts (Archivo, Big Shoulders Display, and Fraunces italic 900, opsz 9, SOFT 50, WONK 1). three.js r128 ships with the site.
- **The game:** plays end to end: 24 tour events, a daily round, quick rounds, a pro shop, scorecards and a local save. The save key is still `tempo-golf-v1`.

## Files

| `index.html`, `sw.js`, `manifest.webmanifest`, `three.min.js`, `icons/`, `splash/`, `.nojekyll` | The site GitHub Pages serves: the game as an installable home-screen app. Generated by `build.py`; don't edit by hand. |

| `test/` | Node and Playwright checks (see Testing). |

## engine.js

### Course generation
- `makeRound(seed, tier, courseIdx)` produces 6 holes from a par pattern.
- `buildHole(cfg)` is deterministic from the seed. It builds:
  - a bezier centreline, with doglegs on par 4s and 5s;
  - a fairway half-width function `fwHalf(s)` and an elliptical green with a fringe;
  - bunkers, ponds and trees (`round`, `pine`, `shrub` or `cactus`) with collision data;
  - a pin on a low-slope spot, plus wind and stimp.
- Each hole exposes `height`, `surface` (TEE, FAIRWAY, ROUGH, SAND, GREEN, FRINGE, WATER, OB), `normal`, `centerInfo`, `greenQ`, `ellQ`, `pondAt` and `treesNear`.
- Difficulty is `tier` (1 to about 9.3). It narrows fairways, adds hazards, and raises wind, green speed and green slope.

### Ball physics (`Ball`, fixed step `DT = 1/240`)
- **Air:** RK2 with drag and Magnus lift. Wind scales with height.
- **Bounce and roll:** the per-surface `SP` table holds restitution `e`, impact friction `mu`, turf loss, rolling resistance, spin bite `grip`, and the lie penalties.
  - Round 4 green row: e 0.38, mu 0.90, grip 1.45. A full 7-iron hops about 0.67 m and runs about 5 m; wedges hop about 0.65 m and check.
  - On greens, rolling resistance comes from stimp.
- **Cup:** `CUP_R = 0.0675` (1.25× regulation since round 4).
  - The ball falls while its centre is inside the cup. It holes once it has dropped more than one ball radius.
  - The back wall can capture it; otherwise it lips out.
  - `holeOut` stores `ball.sink`.
- **Trees (`TREE`, round 4):**
  - **Trunk:** a hard bounce (normal restitution `trunkE` 0.5, then ×`trunkKeep` 0.7).
  - **Canopy:** a bouncy surface. The velocity reflects about the outward normal (a sphere for round trees, a cone for pines), keeping 0.3 of the normal speed and 0.55 of the tangential speed. Then it is turned by up to 8° (seeded), spin is halved, and the ball is pushed out.
  - The same tree is ignored for 0.25 s after a hit.
  - Each hit emits one `tree` event `{ hard, vin, vout, … }`.
- **Calibration:** `calibrate()` sets each club's head speed so a 100% swing carries its target (DR 245 … LW 68 yd), and builds the swing meter's linear-in-carry tables.
- `shotParams(club, power, dev, lie, upgrades)` turns power, path and lie into launch conditions.
- `puttSpeed(hole, d)`: the speed that rolls d m on this green when flat. `puttMax` (the old stroke-length steps) is no longer used by the game (round 6).

## game.js

### State machine (`G.mode`)
`menu → intro → aim ⇄ swing → impact → flight → settle → (aim | holed → card) → results`

### Input and the swing
- **One gesture rule (round 6, `FEEL.gesture`, `pressDecide`):** a one-finger press anywhere but a button (the top cards and the flight strip pass touches through) waits for 10 px of movement. Within 35° of straight down it's a swing, with the press point as the start line; the samples so far are replayed into the swing (`startSwing` → `swingMove`), so power and path are exactly as before. Anything else is an aim drag: only the sideways part turns the aim (`FEEL.aim.sens`, putter `puttSens`); on release the aim coasts (speed over the last 80 ms, at most 1.4 rad/s, e^(−t/0.06 s), stopped at 0.3 s). A tap does nothing at address. Taps in flight / settle / holed are unchanged (hold to fast-forward, skip).
  - Drag down for power, then flick up past the start line. `swingLen` is 25% of the viewport height, clamped to 140–260 px, wherever the press is; a swing started too low stops gaining power at the bottom edge. Pulling past 100% (to 112%) adds error.
  - While pulling, the lie card shows the power and the carry (putts: the flat roll and the last putt on this hole) (`updatePowerCard`). Nothing is drawn over the play area except the start line and the fan.
  - **Chevron (`FEEL.hint`, `chevronAt`):** cream, under the ball at every address, raised if a full pull from it wouldn't fit; "PULL DOWN · FLICK UP" under it for the first 3 shots.
  - **Aim beam (`FEEL.beam`, `drawBeam`):** a soft cream ribbon along the aim while it turns and 0.6 s after, then a 0.3 s fade. It ends in the yellow landing ring when the Landing guide is on (40 m long with it off; just past the cup on putts). Nothing else is drawn on the course at rest or in the swing.
- **Path (round 5, `FEEL.path`):**
  - f = tan φ, φ = the flick's angle from straight up, from the finger's lowest point to where it crosses the start line (right positive, `flickTan`). The pull's drift and length don't count. β (press → lowest point) is recorded only (`G.beta`).
  - `pathMap(f)` is 0 inside ±0.04. Outside it, it's sign(f)·(|f| − 0.04)/0.84, clamped to ±1.6.
  - The same `dev` drives full shots, putts, and the club's sideways tilt (low-passed over 60 ms, from 12 px of rise).
  - PURE needs |f| ≤ 0.05 (2.86°) plus the power, tempo and lie rules (`takeShot`).
  - The fan (`drawFan`) shows the STRAIGHT window (±8°) and the PURE band above the ball during full swings.
- **Club model:** the club lives in the swing plane as an arm angle `a` plus a wrist hinge `b`. `stepClub` runs at 240 Hz substeps inside `updateClub`.
  - **Backswing:** a spring follows the finger (ω 16, ζ 1.0; putter 13). The wrists set between 10% and 72% of the backswing.
  - **Downswing (round 4, non-putters):** it starts on the first sample 3 px above the lowest point. From there the club runs on its own clock, not on the finger's position:
    - angle = a0·(1 − τ²), plus the backswing's last motion decaying over 40 ms.
    - τ advances at (1 − τ)/(T_est − t).
    - `T_est` is the predicted time for the finger to reach the start line (`fingerCross`, a least-squares quadratic through every sample since the lowest point) plus 25 ms, low-passed over 30 ms.
    - A new finger sample changes the pace, never the position.
    - Wrist release lags: `b ∝ (a/aS)^0.5`.
  - **Commit:** when the finger crosses, a cubic Hermite carries the club into the ball at matched speed, within `commit.max` 32 ms.
  - **Contact:** the time already past contact inside its 240 Hz substep is carried into the follow-through. The ball starts at the exact time since contact.
  - **Follow-through:** it starts at the club's impact speed (`follow.nMax` 14).
  - **Putter:** springs on finger progress (unchanged since round 1).
- **Contact dip (`FEEL.dip`):** the time scale eases from 1 to its minimum (0.5 at full power, 0.8 at 30%) over 60 ms, then back to 1 over 0.3 s. No frame changes it by more than 0.15. Putts get no dip.
- **Shaft flex (drawing only, `FEEL.flex`, round 5):** a spring driven by the angular acceleration. The load is released 46 ms before contact as timed by the downswing's own clock (T_est on the τ curve, then the commit), stays off 80 ms after contact, then returns over 120 ms. A toe-down droop out of the swing plane is 0.5 × the bend. Peak tip deflection: driver ~176 mm, 7-iron ~86 mm.

### Club models (`buildClubModel`)
- Flat-shaded Lambert with vertex colours, palette colours only, three meshes per club (grip, shaft, head).
- **Driver and woods (round 4):** a pear head drawn ×1.35 (`FEEL.club.wood`):
  - a 15-point outline with 6 face segments;
  - cream2 sole, cream top-line, green2 crown with a yellow alignment mark;
  - a dark face with a slight bulge, and a hosel.
- **Irons and wedges:** sheared blade profiles.
- **Putter:** a mallet, drawn at 0.6 height so the ball shows at address.
- The contact point is on the face at ball-centre height.

### Camera
- A critically damped spring on the position and look target (`updateCamera`). FOV springs with it.
- **Address (`ADDR`, round 6):** framed like the reference. Standard: 9.61 m back, 1.12 m up on the target line, FOV 40 (the same for every club, so each spans by its real length): ball centred 66.4% down, horizon 50.5%, a 7-iron 13.2% of the height. The ball is drawn at least 7 px (`addrBallK`, up to 1.5×). `down` is solved so the ball sits at `ADDR.ballY` (`solveDown`).
  - **Presets (`camPreset`, `save.settings.cam`, the camera pill above pause):** Close (1/1.4 the distance), Standard, High (same distance, horizon at 37.5%); the ball stays put. Putts: Close brings the putting camera 1/1.4 nearer, High raises it 1.7×.
  - **Yaw spring (`stepCamYaw`, `FEEL.aim.camOmega`):** the address camera turns round the ball after the aim, so the ball and club stay put while the aim is dragged and the beam leans. Once arrived the camera is held exactly on its target (`G.camLock`).
  - In the downswing the whole club stays on screen for 7I–LW; the longer clubs leave the left edge early in the downswing (round 6 report).
- **Look mode (round 6, `lookCam`, `FEEL.look`):** the look button (right edge, on the horizon) flies the camera 0.9 s to above the club's full-power landing spot (putts: the whole green); one finger pans, two pinch; the HUD hides; the grid shows with a depth bias. Tapping again flies back in 0.5 s. The map is look-only too (pan / pinch via `G.view`).
- **Flight tilt (round 5, `flightTilt`):** while a full shot is in the air the camera tilts up just enough to keep the ball `FEEL.cam.flightTopPad` px below the HUD cards, then eases back.
- **Putting (`puttCam`):** shared by address and the putt roll. It backs off and rises with the putt length up to 4 m, so the ball stays ≥12 CSS px on 390×844.
- **Flight, settle, intro and map:** `flightCam`, `mapTarget`, intro lerp. Render interpolation (`G.ip`, `G.bd`) draws the ball one physics step behind.

### Ball, cup and hole-out
- **Ball size:** drawn at 1.0× on the green and during every putt. Off the green it is capped up to 1.5× (blended across the fringe) so it stays visible. At address (full clubs) it is at least 7 px, up to 1.5× (round 6).
- **Putting (round 6, `FEEL.putt`):** one curve: flat roll = 24 m × p² (p = pull / swingLen, to 1.12), through `E.puttSpeed` for the green's stimp. No tempo penalty on putts. After a miss the toast says how far long or short along the start-to-cup line.
- **Hole-out (round 4, `FEEL.holeOut`):**
  - When a rolling ball crosses the rim, a copy is stepped ahead. If it will hole, the drop starts there with the ball's own speed.
  - It then falls under gravity in 1 ms steps, with the wall, the flagstick and the bottom as bounces.
  - It's still within 0.6 s.
  - The first contact plays `cup`; later ones play `rattle`.

### World and visuals
- `paintCourse()` paints each hole into a canvas texture in world metres. Terrain is a smooth-shaded grid; the green is its own finer grid with a height tint.
- **Green relief (round 5, drawing only):** the green is drawn at `hVis = h + (relief − 1)·w·(h − pin.y)` (`FEEL.green.relief` 2.5, w = the tint weight). The ball, shadow, club root, dots, grid, aim line, hole-out and cameras use the drawn surface; a ball fades out of the offset between 0.3 and 1.0 m up (`visOff`). The slope grid is cream screen-space ribbons every 0.9 m. Physics never sees it.
- Vegetation is instanced, with shader wind sway. The sky dome and backdrop rings are styled by course. Water uses a custom shader.
- **Rough lie (round 4):** one soft ellipse decal, 0.36 × 0.28 m and 12% darker (`FEEL.lie`).
  - While the ball rests in the rough it is drawn 25% of its diameter lower, and the decal stands in for its contact shadow.
  - The decal goes as soon as the ball moves.
  - There are no grass tufts any more.
- **Flag:** two-sided cloth with a per-hole canvas texture. The G$ mark (G in Fraunces, $ from the club paths) is 60% of the flag's height and reads correctly from both sides. It waves with the wind.
- **Tracer:** a glow ribbon plus a core ribbon, with no edge. Yellow core for PURE, cream otherwise. It holds 0.7 s after rest, then fades over 0.6 s.

### HUD and screens (`game.src.html`)
- Cream cards on the course; the PURE toast. No swing meter (round 5).
- **Flight (round 6):** from contact to the next address five small stat cards (`#hudStats`: clubhead speed = launch ball speed ÷ `FEEL.stats.smash`, power, back β, through φ, tempo; putts: power in ft and through) replace the top cards. A distance label rides above the ball (`drawDistLabel`, `FEEL.label`). A fast-forward button (hold) at the top right while the ball moves. `hudVis` decides what shows.
- **Transient cards (round 5):** the toast and the popup sit in the band under the HUD cards (top inset + 166 px), one at a time (`hideTransients`). The contact toast shows above the bottom buttons while the ball is in the air (`#toast.low`). Toasts clear when the next address starts.
- **Version label:** `#verLabel` on the main menu, bottom right. Bump it every round.
- **Score popup (`#banner`, round 4):** the PURE toast's card, with a yellow underline under par and a warn one over par. The hole-number intro uses the same element.
- **Hole card and results (`#scrCard`, `#scrResults`, round 4):**
  - a cream panel on a dim green backdrop;
  - Fraunces numbers, Archivo labels;
  - chip score cells: yellow for birdie or better, cream2 with warn text for bogey or worse, a ring for double;
  - fits 360 px wide.
- Colours come from `PALETTE` in game.js and matching CSS variables: cream `#F3ECD8`, cream2 `#E4DAC0`, green `#1F3A2A`, green2 `#2F5641`, yellow `#E3B93F`, warn `#B94529`.

### Audio (WebAudio, no files, no oscillators in the impacts)
- Impacts are a short noise burst through resonant bandpass modes (`FEEL.sound`), by club class, plus turf, sand and rough layers. There is also a whoosh.
- **Round 4:** `canopy` (a wooden bonk with a quiet leaf swish), `trunk`, `cup` and `rattle`.
  - One tree sound per hit, never two within 0.25 s.

### Save and progression
- `localStorage` key `tempo-golf-v1`: coins, upgrades, tour records, daily bests, stats, settings and an in-progress round.
- `loadSave` merges onto defaults, so older saves load unchanged (round 6 adds `settings.cam`, default 'std'). Saves from rounds 1–5 are tested (`savecompat6.js`, with the round 3, 4 and 5 builds as writers).
- **Screen (round 6):** in the home-screen app (standalone), `html` gets `min-height: min(100lvh, calc(100% + env(safe-area-inset-top)))` against WebKit bug 301108 (iOS 26 lays a translucent-status-bar app out one status bar short). `resize()` sizes everything from `#app` on resize, orientation, visual viewport and a ResizeObserver. Pause → Perf shows the sizes and insets (`sizeInfo`).

## Publishing (every round)

**Every round ends with these steps (from round 5):**
1. Bump `#verLabel`, run `python3 build.py`.
2. Push to `claude/round-N`, merge it into `main` as one "Round N" commit. No tag. The sandbox can't delete branches (403); leave them and say so.
   - From this sandbox only `claude/*` branches can be pushed, so the merge goes through a pull request squash-merged with `env -u GH_TOKEN gh api` (create: `POST repos/thazerk12/gstick-golf/pulls`; merge: `PUT …/pulls/N/merge` with `merge_method=squash`).
3. Wait for the Pages deploy (`gh api repos/thazerk12/gstick-golf/actions/runs`) and confirm https://thazerk12.github.io/gstick-golf/ serves the new `sw.js` build hash.
4. Update the claude.ai artifact as a fallback, and save the report to the project.

**What the site is:**
- `index.html` with iOS home-screen tags: full screen, a black-translucent status bar, the G$ icon, and launch screens for 6.1", 6.3" and 6.9" iPhones.
- `manifest.webmanifest`, the icons (180, 192, 512, maskable 512), and a local `three.min.js` (r128), so there's no CDN dependency.
- `sw.js`, the service worker:
  - The page is network first: a new round shows up on the next online launch, and the cached copy is used offline or after 4 s.
  - Everything else comes from the cache. Google Fonts are cached on first load.
  - The cache name is a hash of the whole build, so every build replaces the old cache.
- `test/app4.js` checks install, offline play and the update path.
- **Saves don't move between sites.** They live in each site's own browser storage, under the same key `tempo-golf-v1`.

## Known gaps and next candidates

- **Real-hardware feel is unverified.** Every check runs headless in SwiftShader. The iPhone try-list in the latest report is the way in.
- **The long clubs leave the screen in the downswing** (round 6 framing): DR–6I keep the whole club on screen for 26–75% of downswing frames (7I–LW 98–100%). The club reaches farthest left early in the downswing. Fixing it needs a tighter drawn swing plane for long clubs or a farther camera for them.
- **The green relief banks at the fringe:** the green's edge is drawn up to 1.2 m off the real ground and the fringe ramps it back. Lower `FEEL.green.relief` if it reads badly.
- **Next (from the round 6 brief):** menus and the other screens (round 7), the course (round 8).
- **No golfer avatar.** The club pivots from an invisible swing centre.
- **Right-handed only.** No multiplayer or accounts, and saves stay in one browser.
